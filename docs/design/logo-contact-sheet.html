What the readout measures

ink bbox
Union of getBBox() over every painted element, widened by half the stroke width wherever a stroke is actually painted. In the candidate's own user units — check the viewBox line before reading it as artboard units. On a stroked mark a second, geometry-only box is printed beside it: the widening is exact at a round or square cap and up to w/2 too long at a butt-capped open end, so the true ink lies between the two. A filled mark has no gap between them.
live area
construction.md: geometry occupies 16 … 240 on both axes. Overshoot is allowed to spill outward by its own amount; nothing else is.
nodes
Every M, L, H, V, A endpoint and every cubic/quadratic endpoint. Bézier control points are not nodes. An endpoint coincident with its subpath start is counted once. Ceilings from reproduction.md: 28 per contour, 56 per file, 24 is the redraw signal.
coord reuse
Distinct ÷ total coordinate values, per path, computed only at 12 or more values. Coordinates are endpoint and control-point coordinates, plus an arc's rx and ry — reproduction.md counts the radii because a shared radius is the clearest evidence of derivation there is. An arc's x-axis-rotation and its two flags are not coordinates and are excluded. Above 0.75 is reproduction.md's tracing signal.
text
advance edge is the right edge of getBBox(). ink edge is that edge with the trailing letter-spacing step removed — whether this renderer applies one is measured, by re-measuring the same run at zero tracking and counting the steps in the difference, not assumed. fit × is the multiplier on font-size that lands the ink edge on 224; it assumes text-anchor="start" and letter-spacing scaled with the size, per mark-types.md's wordmark recipe.
A text element's getBBox() is its layout box — vertically it runs from the font's ascent to its descent, not from cap height to baseline. Read the vertical extents of a wordmark accordingly; they are not tight ink.
M3 inversion
reproduction.md's mono-collapse M3, computed here — this sheet is the only instrument in the plugin that rasterises, so it is the only place the diff can happen. The candidate is serialised twice with color resolved to #000 and #fff, drawn over white and over black on a 256 px canvas, the second inverted, and the maximum per-channel difference taken. The gate is quoted from reproduction.md beside the number, never restated. Printed with it: the count of pixels above the gate, the count of ink pixels, and the raster's own ink box — so a difference of zero taken over a raster that drew nothing reads as zero-of-nothing rather than as a pass.
A candidate carrying text is diffed twice, whole and again with the text removed. Path antialiasing here is exactly symmetric under inversion; glyph rasterisation is not, and the second run is what measures that rather than assuming it. The drawn geometry is graded; the type's share is recorded unrun, the same posture reproduction.md takes for a text element's extents. Outline the type and the whole file becomes decidable.
Nothing to do with the mono columns below: those are a CSS filter for looking at, this is two renders and a pixel comparison.
paint
The literal fill / stroke attribute values present on painted elements. ⟨absent⟩ matters: construction.md notes that omitting fill does not inherit — SVG's initial fill is black, which silently defeats the currentColor binding.
mono columns
A CSS filter previewing collapse to one value. A viewing aid on this sheet only — never something to copy into a shipped asset, and it cannot model alpha.
